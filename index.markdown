# Translational Hematology

Our lab studies disease onset and progression mechanisms in myeloid hematological neoplasms. We use mainly primary patient material in our translational research program of indepth molecular characterization of disease mechanisms, identifying prognostic biomarkers and and actionable targets for treatment, and we run clinical trials at the hematology clinic, US Linköping.
